SMP Starter Kit Bedrock behavior pack

Import the SmpStarterKit_BP folder as a behavior pack, or compress its contents into a ZIP archive and rename the archive to SmpStarterKit.mcpack.

Activate the behavior pack on the world where the kit should be available. The pack uses the Bedrock Script API. Each player receives one stone sword, stone pickaxe, stone axe, stone shovel, 16 oak logs, 16 bread, and 32 torches on their first spawn. A player tag prevents the kit from being granted again after reconnecting.
